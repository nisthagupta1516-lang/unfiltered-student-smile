CHAPTER 03 — FINDING HER WAY

She deserves more than just coping.

Understanding the pressure is the first step. Learning how to deal with it is the next.

A SMALL REMINDER FOR NAINA

She doesn't need to fix her entire life in one day. She just needs a little support, one step at a time.

01 The Assignment Fear When everything feels too much. +

Too many assignments, too little time, and no idea where to begin.

What might help her?

  • Write down assignments and deadlines.
  • Start with the most urgent task.
  • Break big tasks into smaller steps.
  • Take short breaks while studying.

"You don't have to do everything at once. Just begin with one thing."

02 The Stage Fear When words disappear in front of everyone. +

She knows her topic, but nervousness makes speaking difficult.

What might help her?

  • Prepare key points instead of memorising everything.
  • Practise in front of a mirror or friend.
  • Take a slow breath before speaking.
  • Focus on sharing ideas, not being perfect.

"Confidence grows every time you try."

03 The Marks Pressure When grades define her self-worth. +

One bad result makes her question her abilities and her future.

What might help her?

  • Understand mistakes instead of fearing them.
  • Ask teachers or classmates for help.
  • Set learning goals, not only score-based goals.
  • Remember that one result cannot define her future.

"A marksheet shows a result. It never tells your whole story."

04 The Comparison Trap When everyone seems ahead of her. +

She compares her skills and achievements with other students.

What might help her?

  • Focus on her own goals and progress.
  • Write down small achievements every week.
  • Limit social media if it increases self-doubt.
  • Take inspiration from others without comparing.

"Someone else's progress doesn't make your journey less meaningful."

05 The Weight of Expectations When she fears disappointing her family. +

She wants to make her family proud but struggles to express her feelings.

What might help her?

  • Talk to someone she trusts.
  • Explain her challenges honestly and calmly.
  • Ask for specific support and guidance.
  • Remember that needing help is not failure.

"She can love her family and still need space to understand herself."

06 The 2 AM Thoughts When the world is quiet but her mind isn't. +

She worries about failure, her future and whether she is good enough.

What might help her?

  • Write down worries instead of keeping them inside.
  • Breathe gently and slowly.
  • Focus on one small thing she can do now.
  • Talk to someone trusted if worries continue.

"Not every question needs an answer tonight. Some things can wait until morning."

PLEASE REMEMBER

She doesn't need to be perfect.
She needs room to be human.

Small steps matter. Rest matters. Asking for help matters. No student should have to carry everything alone.
